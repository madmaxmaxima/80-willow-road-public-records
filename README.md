# 80 Willow Road — Public Records Compilation

Research compiled from public records about the proposed Willow Park development at
80 Willow Road, Menlo Park, California, and about the ownership and political
relationships surrounding it.

## Background

N17 Development (founded June 2023 by Oisin Heneghan, formerly a Trammell Crow
vice-president) is the applicant for a 665-unit, 458-foot mixed-use project on the
6.7-acre site of the former *Sunset* magazine headquarters. The property is held by
Willow Project LLC, whose owner is reported as Vitaly Yusufov, son of Igor Yusufov,
Russia's energy minister from 2001 to 2004 and a former board member of Rosneft,
Transneft and Gazprom.

Reporting cited in this compilation states that Deutsche Bank's compliance function
initially declined the 2018 purchase over reputational and sanctions concerns before
a global committee overruled the objection, and that Vitaly Yusufov is subject to
Ukrainian sanctions.

On 29 July 2026 the California Attorney General issued a notice to Menlo Park under
AB 712 concerning AB 2011 and the Housing Accountability Act. On 3 August 2026 the
developer's counsel delivered a notice of intent to sue the city.
