### Basis and Public Interest

The Attorney General's July 29, 2026 AB 712 notice to Menlo Park is a consequential enforcement action that could expose the city to mandatory fines of $10,000 per housing unit ($6.65 million for 665 units) if a court later finds the city violated state housing law after receiving the AG's warning. This is the first known AB 712 notice directed at a city's processing of a specific development application, as distinguished from prior AG enforcement against cities that failed to adopt compliant housing elements. The notice was issued to a city with an HCD-certified housing element, making it qualitatively different from every prior housing enforcement action.

Understanding who prompted this enforcement action (whether it originated from the AG's own monitoring, from HCD referral, or from the developer or its representatives) is essential to evaluating whether the enforcement mechanism is being used as intended or being weaponized as a litigation strategy. This question has taken on added urgency following the Del Mar City Council's August 2026 vote requesting the San Diego County District Attorney and U.S. Attorney investigate AG Bonta over $36,400 in campaign contributions linked to the Seaside Ridge housing development ($18,200 from Brian Guiltinan on Seaside Ridge team plus $18,200 from Mark Arabo, who hired Guiltinan), another case where the AG intervened on behalf of a specific 259-unit project after receiving developer-connected donations. The Del Mar referral is the first formal criminal referral by a California city against its own AG over housing enforcement, establishing a pattern that Menlo Park now joins.

The developer behind 80 Willow is N17 Development (founder Oisin Heneghan, former SVP at Trammell Crow, founded N17 in June 2023 with no prior portfolio to file the largest Builder's Remedy project in Bay Area history). The property owner is Willow Project LLC, controlled by Vitaly Yusufov, son of Igor Yusufov (former Russian Energy Minister under Putin 2001-2004, Gazprom board 2003-2013, Rosneft board, Forbes $850M, subject to Ukrainian sanctions through October 2032). The $72 million purchase triggered a Deutsche Bank suspicious activity report to FinCEN after the bank's New York office initially rejected the transaction as a politically exposed person risk. Whether the AG conducted ownership due diligence before deploying brand-new enforcement authority for this specific developer is central to public accountability.

### Exemption Anticipation and Privilege Log

I recognize that item 3 may produce records subject to the deliberative process privilege (Government Code § 7927.500), attorney-client privilege, or pending litigation (Government Code § 7927.705). To the extent any records are withheld, I request a privilege log identifying each withheld record by date, author, recipient, subject matter, and the specific exemption claimed. If § 7927.705 is claimed, please identify the specific litigation and explain how disclosure would prejudice it per Fairley v. Superior Court (1998) 66 Cal.App.4th 1414.

I note that items 1, 2, and 4 request records of external communications and complaints, which are not subject to the deliberative process privilege. Factual material that is reasonably segregable from deliberative content must be disclosed per Government Code § 7922.575. Communications with external parties who have an independent financial interest adverse to the public cannot be withheld as common-interest privileged.

Pursuant to City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608, this request includes communications on personal devices, personal email accounts, and messaging applications (including Signal, WhatsApp, and text messages) where such communications relate to public business concerning the 80 Willow Road project or the AB 712 notice. Please search personal devices and accounts of custodians likely to have responsive communications, including the Attorney General, Chief Deputy AG, and Housing Strike Force leadership.

I request that responsive records be provided in electronic format with any redactions narrowly tailored and accompanied by a justification for each redaction.
