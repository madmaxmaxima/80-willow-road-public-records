### Basis and Public Interest

Understanding the corporate structure and beneficial ownership of the entities behind the proposed 80 Willow Road mega-development in Menlo Park is a matter of significant public interest. The 80 Willow site is a 665-unit, 458-foot tower on 1.5 acres, the largest Builder's Remedy style project in the Bay Area, filed by N17 Development (Oisin Heneghan, former SVP at Trammell Crow, founded N17 June 2023 with no prior portfolio) for a property owned by Willow Project LLC, controlled by Vitaly Yusufov, son of Igor Yusufov (former Russian Energy Minister 2001-2004 under Putin, Gazprom board 2003-2013, Rosneft board, Forbes $850M, Ukrainian sanctions through October 2032, Canada sanctions, Q4535427, Pandora Papers / ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database 6 BVI shells, OCCRP Croatian island resort 37.4M euro investigation).

The property's $72 million purchase triggered a Deutsche Bank suspicious activity report to FinCEN after the bank's New York office initially rejected the transaction as a politically exposed person risk. The developer files applications for both Willow Park LLC and Allrise Capital Inc., an Irvine-based investment firm with a portfolio of $500M+ in Bay Area real estate (Canopic Advisory, Garrett Camporine, Ruslan Zinurov, Mikhail Trubchik). Mapping the full corporate structure, including any shared ownership, management, or financial connections between these entities, is essential for evaluating transparency, potential sanctions evasion, and whether state enforcement power under AB 712 is being deployed for the benefit of a Ukrainian-sanctioned individual 5 miles from NASA Ames/Moffett Field, which has had indefinite CFIUS review authority since NDAA §8102.

Delaware corporate records (where Willow Project LLC is organized) are not subject to California PRA, but California Secretary of State filings for entities doing business in California are. This request is limited to California filings retrievable through existing electronic search interfaces.
