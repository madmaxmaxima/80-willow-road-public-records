## Lefortovo Military Land Seizure (Discovered August 7, 2026)

### Summary

Multiple Russian investigative outlets (Antimaf, InView, FactInFace, Kartoteka) published detailed investigations in May-June 2026 revealing that the Yusufov family illegally acquired 18 Ministry of Defense land plots in Moscow's Lefortovo district through offshore structures and political connections.

### Key Facts

1. **The property:** 18 land plots in Tankovy Proezd, Lefortovo, Moscow, formerly belonging to the 101st Central Automobile Repair Plant (FGUP "101 TsARZ"), which had operated on the site since the 1940s. Includes facilities of the Prince Alexander Nevsky Military University, including a swimming pool where cadets train.

2. **The scheme (2005-2012):**
 - 2005: Federal Agency for State Property Management (Rosimushchestvo) assigned 68 real estate properties to the military enterprise
 - Sept 2008: President Dmitry Medvedev signed decree to corporatize Ministry of Defense FGUPs
 - Defense Minister Anatoly Serdyukov and his assistant Evgenia Vasilyeva facilitated the transfer
 - Vasilyeva installed classmate Maksim Zakutaylo as director of 101 TsARZ
 - In 2012, Vasilyeva directed one of her employees to become director of TsARZ and three other MOD firms, after which property was siphoned off
 - "Investment and Construction Company 'MSU Territory Development Fund'" (ISK FORT) appeared among TsARZ founders, registered to **Cypriot offshore DISCROMATON INVESTMENTS LIMITED** (managed by Cypriot nominees: Christina Demosthenous, Michalis Spyroulla, Elena Lou)
 - Igor Pyshkin became ISK FORT director (later reappeared in 2018 at Dom.rf auction representing LLC "Citizen")
 - Land reclassified from "defense" to "commercial development" in violation of Russian law prohibiting such withdrawal from state use

3. **SZ Tankovy chain:**
 - 2012: Properties transferred to LLC "Tankovy" (later renamed "SZ Tankovy")
 - Founded by another Cypriot offshore, **GELSTEN INVESTMENTS LIMITED** (99%), and Anatoly Sergeev (1%), who had worked in the Building Operation Directorate of the MFA
 - After 2022 de-offshorization, Vitaly Yusufov became direct owner of SZ Tankovy and LLC Citizen
 - Summer 2024: Vitaly formally exited, replaced by LLC "SZ Yuzhnoye More" (registered to Krasnodar entrepreneurs Nikolay Shikhidi and Sofia Toros)
 - Experts suggest Shikhidi/Toros are nominees and Yusufov retains actual control

4. **Forced rent payments:** The Ministry of Defense has been paying rent to Yusufov-controlled entities for its own facilities on the land for over 10 years. A recent bill for 25 million rubles arrived.

5. **Nochnoy Katok (co-conspirators):** Part of the Tankovy plots went to LLC "Nochnoy Katok," registered to Andrey Loginov (since 2019), who is in fact a senior manager at restaurateur Andrey Dellos's companies. Loginov is described as an "obvious nominee" who received no salary from Nochnoy Katok despite it owning land worth hundreds of millions of rubles.

6. **Alexander Chistyakov connection:** Husband of singer Glyukoza, long-time friend and business partner of Igor Yusufov. Together they invested in Ruspetro (oil and gas). Chistyakov's family transferred business to the same Krasnodar nominees (Shikhidi/Toros) through lawyer Yuri Pustovit.

7. **Current legal action:** Moscow City Military Prosecutor's Office has initiated a case in Arbitration Court to return the 18 land plots to the state. If successful, it would be precedent for all development projects on former military lands in central Moscow that went through offshore schemes between 2008-2012.
