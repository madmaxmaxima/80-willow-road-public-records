## Headline

**The council took no action on 80 Willow.** The closed-session report-out was
"no reportable action," and 80 Willow appeared on no public agenda item. But the
meeting's first ~50 minutes were a remarkable event the city's own chapter
markers omit: **19 residents used the pre-closed-session public comment period
to lobby the council on 80 Willow**, overwhelmingly urging the city to fight
the project, the developer, and AG Bonta's letter, including calls for
litigation, a federal money-laundering inquiry, and even a Bonta recall.

## What the agenda actually contained

- C1: anticipated litigation (one case, Gov Code 54956.9(d)(2))
- C2: existing litigation (**Mircea Voskerician and 12 Maywood LLC v. City of
 Menlo Park, Case No. 26-CIV-05627)** (this is the Voskerician/PLF-adjacent suit
 in the campaign's corpus, NOT an 80 Willow case)
- C3: labor negotiations (unrepresented management)
- Regular session: proclamations, consent, Coleman Ave pilot (J2), advisory work
 plans (J1), Axon public-safety platform (J3), SEIU successor agreement (J4),
 salary schedule (J5). No 80 Willow item anywhere on the public agenda.
